Who we serve

Built for organisations at every stage.

Different organisations need different things from their advisers. We tailor our approach to your size, sector and ambitions.

Large corporates & groups

Multi-entity groups that need robust group reporting, technical accounting support and an auditor who understands complexity.

  • Group and component audits
  • Consolidated financial statements
  • IFRS technical support
  • ECL and complex judgement areas

Mid-sized & family businesses

Growing businesses that want practical advice, strong controls and a partner who is available when needed.

  • Statutory audit and tax compliance
  • Internal control improvements
  • Budgeting and forecasting
  • Succession and restructuring support

Startups & venture-backed companies

Founders who need to be audit-ready early and meet the reporting expectations of investors. We act as appointed statutory auditor to the startup portfolio of one of Egypt's leading venture investors.

  • First statutory audits
  • Investor and board reporting
  • Share-based payments and funding instruments
  • Due diligence readiness

International & Gulf clients

Multinational subsidiaries, Gulf-based groups and foreign investors who need a reliable partner in Egypt.

  • Reporting to foreign parents
  • Treaty relief and transfer pricing
  • KSA accounting and consolidation
  • Market-entry guidance

Accounting & advisory firms

Peer firms that need specialist technical capacity or experienced back-office support.

  • ECL model validation and preparation
  • Back-office accounting support
  • Reporting quality control
  • Technical consultations
