Privacy policy

How we handle information you share with us through this website.

ASA Chartered Accountants and Consultants ("ASA", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information submitted through asaegypt.com.

Information we collect

When you contact us through the website, we collect the information you provide — such as your name, company, email address, phone number and message.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the information you request.
  • To assess and prepare proposals for professional services.
  • To comply with professional and legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the website and handle enquiries, and where required by law or professional regulation.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only as long as necessary for the purposes above, and apply appropriate safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have provided by emailing ahmed.sayed@asaegypt.com.

Client confidentiality

Information relating to client engagements is governed by our professional confidentiality obligations and engagement terms, not by this website policy.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
