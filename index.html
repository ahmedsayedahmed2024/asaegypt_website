Our specialist edge

IFRS 9 expertise trusted by international network firms.

Expected Credit Loss is where judgement, data and modelling meet — and where auditors and regulators look hardest. We design, prepare and independently validate ECL models that are well-documented, reconciled and ready for scrutiny.
