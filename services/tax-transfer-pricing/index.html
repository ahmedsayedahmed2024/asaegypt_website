Overview

From compliance to controversy — one team.

Egyptian tax rules continue to evolve, and inspections increasingly focus on documentation: intercompany transactions, withholding, VAT and the support behind every deduction.

We help clients stay compliant, plan with confidence and defend their positions — combining audit discipline with practical insight into how inspections are conducted in practice.

For groups, we prepare transfer pricing documentation and studies that are consistent with international guidance and with the realities of local review.
